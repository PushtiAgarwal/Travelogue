FAQ

  • What are the means of transport to reach Tokyo from Mumbai?

    +

    The main means of transport from Mumbai to Tokyo are flights, often with layovers in cities like Bangkok or Hong Kong, followed by trains from Japanese airports to central Tokyo.

  • Which delicacies can be enjoyed by vegetarians in Tokyo?

    +

    Vegetarians in Tokyo can enjoy dishes like vegetable ramen, tofu-based meals, vegetarian sushi, and traditional Buddhist cuisine known as shojin ryori.

  • Which is the ideal season to explore Tokyo?

    +

    The ideal seasons to explore Tokyo are spring-March to May for cherry blossoms and mild weather, and autumn-September to November for vibrant foliage and comfortable temperatures.

  • What are the top destinations to visit in Japan besides Tokyo?

    +

    Beyond Tokyo, top destinations in Japan include Kyoto for its temples, Osaka for street food, Nara for deer and history, Hiroshima for peace memorials, and Sapporo for snow festivals.

  • Can I use a JR Pass for travel across all major cities in Japan?

    +

    Yes, the JR Pass covers most major cities in Japan via the extensive JR network and Shinkansen lines, though it excludes Nozomi and Mizuho services unless you purchase a supplemental ticket.
